# EduVerse AI
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

EduVerse AI is an AI-powered education and career platform designed to support students throughout their academic and professional journey.

Instead of treating education, career guidance, skill development, and opportunities as separate products, EduVerse connects them into one personalized ecosystem.

The platform adapts to the student's education level.

### Below Undergraduate

**Learning + Academic Planning + Career Discovery**

### Undergraduate & Above

**Learning + Skills + Career + Opportunities**

EduVerse will combine structured educational resources, AI-powered study assistance, personalized study planning, career roadmaps, skill-gap analysis, scholarships, internships, jobs, communities, contests, events, hackathons, and engagement features.

A dedicated Teacher Portal will provide a separate experience for teachers, with role-based access and tools for supporting and monitoring students.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Students frequently use multiple disconnected platforms for:

- Learning concepts
- Finding study material
- Planning their studies
- Asking doubts
- Understanding career options
- Developing skills
- Finding scholarships
- Finding internships/jobs
- Discovering hackathons and contests
- Connecting with peers

This creates several problems:

1. Students do not know what they should learn next.
2. Students struggle to create realistic study plans.
3. Career decisions are often made without enough information.
4. Students may not understand the skills required for a target career.
5. There is a gap between academic learning and career preparation.
6. Opportunities are scattered across many platforms.
7. Generic AI assistants do not have enough educational context about the student.
8. Teachers lack a unified system for managing learning and understanding student progress.

EduVerse aims to connect these disconnected activities into one personalized platform.

---

# 3. Product Vision

> **Build an intelligent education ecosystem that understands where a student is, where they want to go, and continuously helps them move toward that goal.**

EduVerse should answer:

### What should I learn?
→ Learning Hub + AI Study Assistant

### How should I learn it?
→ Study Planner + Personalized Learning

### What career could I pursue?
→ Career Discovery + Career Guidance

### How do I reach that career?
→ Career Roadmapper

### What am I missing?
→ Skill Gap Analyzer

### Where can I apply what I've learned?
→ Projects + Certifications + Opportunities

# 4. Goals

## Primary Goals

- Provide personalized educational assistance.
- Help students plan their academic work.
- Help students discover suitable careers.
- Provide structured career roadmaps.
- Identify skill gaps between current abilities and target careers.
- Connect learning with career outcomes.
- Provide relevant scholarships and opportunities.
- Create an engaging learning environment.
- Provide teachers with tools to support students.
- Use AI as an intelligent assistant rather than simply a generic chatbot.

## Secondary Goals

- Encourage consistent learning habits.
- Increase student engagement.
- Help students discover opportunities earlier.
- Create communities around learning and careers.
- Build a long-term student profile that improves recommendations.

---

# 5. Non-Goals

The initial product will NOT attempt to:

- Replace teachers entirely.
- Replace universities or schools.
- Guarantee career outcomes.
- Make irreversible career decisions for students.
- Treat AI responses as authoritative without appropriate context.
- Build a proprietary foundation LLM initially.
- Build every planned feature simultaneously.

# 11. AI Study Assistant

The AI Study Assistant is one of EduVerse's core features.

Students should be able to ask questions conversationally.

The AI should:

- Explain concepts.
- Give examples.
- Simplify difficult topics.
- Break problems into steps.
- Generate practice questions.
- Quiz students.
- Adapt explanations to student level.
- Explain mistakes.
- Provide learning guidance.

The AI should avoid giving unnecessarily advanced explanations to beginners.

The student's education level and context should influence the response.

# 17. Skill Gap Analyzer

The Skill Gap Analyzer compares a student's current abilities against the requirements of a target career.

Example:

```text
Target Career: Data Scientist

Statistics       ❌
Python           ❌
SQL              ⚠️
Mathematics      ✅
Data Viz         ⚠️
Communication    ✅
```

The system should then:

1. Identify missing skills.
2. Identify partially developed skills.
3. Identify mastered skills.
4. Prioritize important gaps.
5. Recommend resources.
6. Generate a learning path.
7. Track improvement.

Architecture:

```text
Target Career
      ↓
Required Skills
      ↓
Current Student Skills
      ↓
Comparison
      ↓
Skill Gap
      ↓
Prioritized Learning Path
      ↓
Progress
```

# 27. AI Architecture

EduVerse should use external LLM APIs initially.

Potential providers:

- OpenAI
- Google Gemini

The platform should use an abstraction layer:

```text
EduVerse AI Service
        ↓
 ┌──────┴──────┐
 ↓             ↓
OpenAI       Gemini
```

This prevents the entire application from becoming dependent on a single provider.

Potential internal AI functions:

- Explanation generation
- Question generation
- Quiz generation
- Study planning
- Career analysis
- Skill-gap analysis
- Conversational tutoring

# 37. Key Risks

## AI hallucination

AI may provide incorrect educational or career information.

**Mitigation:**
- RAG
- Structured knowledge
- Clear uncertainty handling
- Appropriate source references
- Human oversight where needed

## Over-personalization

Recommendations may become overly narrow.

**Mitigation:**
- Show alternatives
- Encourage exploration
- Allow students to change goals

## Opportunity data becoming outdated

Scholarships, internships, jobs, and events change frequently.

**Mitigation:**
- Reliable external data sources
- Data freshness checks
- Expiration dates
- Periodic ingestion

## Feature overload

EduVerse has a very large scope.

**Mitigation:**
- Modular development
- Clear MVP
- Phased releases
- Avoid building every feature simultaneously

## AI dependency

Dependence on one LLM provider could become expensive or risky.

**Mitigation:**
- AI abstraction layer
- Multiple providers
- Model routing

# 40. Final Product Definition

EduVerse AI is a personalized student ecosystem combining:

**Education + AI + Academic Planning + Career Guidance + Skills + Opportunities + Community + Engagement**

with separate experiences for:

**Students** and **Teachers**.

The defining differentiator should be the connection between these systems.

A student should not have to figure out independently:

> "What should I study?"

> "What career should I choose?"

> "What skills am I missing?"

> "How do I develop those skills?"

> "Where can I use them?"

EduVerse should progressively answer those questions through a single connected experience.
